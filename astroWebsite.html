About Guru Ji

A Divine Journey of Spiritual Wisdom

आचार्य लोकेश शर्मा जी एक प्रसिद्ध ज्योतिषाचार्य, भागवत कथा वाचक और आध्यात्मिक गुरु हैं। 25 वर्षों से अधिक के अनुभव के साथ, उन्होंने हजारों भक्तों के जीवन में सकारात्मक परिवर्तन लाए हैं।

Guru Ji Lokesh Sharma is a renowned astrologer, Bhagwat Katha narrator, and spiritual guide with over 25 years of divine service. Born into a family of Vedic scholars in Bikaner, Rajasthan, he mastered the sacred texts under the guidance of his grandfather.

His expertise spans Vedic Astrology, Kundli Matching, Vastu Shastra, Gemstone Consultation, and Spiritual Healing. Having completed over 500+ Bhagwat Kathas across India, Guru Ji has touched the lives of more than 50,000 devotees.
